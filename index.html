ABOUT THE CONDITION

What Is Metabolic Syndrome?

Metabolic syndrome is a group of health conditions that can increase the risk of cardiovascular disease, type 2 diabetes and other health problems.

IoT-based monitoring can collect physiological data continuously and provide healthcare professionals with useful information about a patient's condition.

Connected sensors can measure blood pressure, glucose, heart rate, oxygen saturation and body temperature and transmit the measurements to a monitoring platform.

MEDICAL IoT DEVICES

Connected Sensors

IoT Monitoring in Practice

A connected medical sensor collects a measurement and sends it to an IoT device or gateway. The gateway can transmit the information to a cloud or server where health data can be stored and viewed by authorized users.

Continuous monitoring can help organize repeated measurements and make changes in physiological parameters easier to notice.

DATA TRANSMISSION

From Sensor to Dashboard

The monitoring workflow starts with physiological measurements. The IoT device receives the readings and sends them through a communication network to a cloud or server platform.

  • Sensor collects a physiological measurement
  • IoT device receives the sensor reading
  • Network transmits the data
  • Cloud/server stores and processes information
  • Dashboard displays the result to authorized users
BENEFITS

Why Continuous Monitoring?

  • Frequent access to physiological measurements
  • Centralized health data
  • Faster visibility of unusual readings
  • Remote monitoring support
  • Better organization of repeated measurements
